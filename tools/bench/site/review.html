Brightline 2 Electric Kettle

Reviewed by Priya N. - 4 out of 5 stars

Photo of the kettle

I have used the Brightline 2 every morning for three months. It boils a full litre in a little under three minutes, which is noticeably faster than my old kettle, and it switches off reliably once it boils. The water window is easy to read and the base does not slide around on the counter.
